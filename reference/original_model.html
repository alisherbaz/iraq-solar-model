Heat temperature derating — Iraq summer impact

Iraqi summers regularly exceed 45°C. Panels lose ~0.4%/°C above 25°C STC. At 55°C ambient, cell temp ~70°C → ~18% power loss. Bifacial panels and elevated mounting reduce this.

Iraq & international financing programmes
Iraq RE Law No. 42 (2023) — Import duty exemption
Solar panels, inverters and mounting hardware fully exempt from import duties and VAT for licensed commercial projects. Apply via MoE renewable energy directorate.
—
World Bank Iraq Financing Facility (IBRD)
Concessional project financing at 3–5% for renewable energy. Up to 70% of capex eligible. Active IDA programme in Iraq 2024–2028. Requires MoE counter-guarantee.
—
IFC / MIGA private sector guarantee
IFC provides partial risk guarantees for private commercial solar. Reduces financing cost by 1–3% and unlocks international debt. Food sector qualifies under agri-value-chain program.
—
UNDP GEF Iraq Energy Efficiency Grant
Direct grants for energy-efficiency + renewable hybrid projects in industrial sector. Typical range $50k–$500k non-repayable. Rolling application cycle.
